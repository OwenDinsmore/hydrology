Each pore is a straight capillary of radius r running the length of the cylinder. At head h every pore with r > r* = −2σ cos γ / (ρw g h) has drained; θ(h) is the remaining pore volume over the cylinder volume. Length cancels in the ratio, so φ = Σ ri² / R². A random subset of pores is drawn in 3D at an exaggerated radius.
