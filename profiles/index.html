ΘE with height above the water table

    Darcy's law is a force balance. For steady flow, the forces on the water per unit weight are gravity (1, down), the suction gradient ds/dz, which pulls water toward drier soil, and drag from the grains, q/K, which always opposes the flow. They balance as

    ds/dz = 1 + q/K(s)

    At rest there is no drag, so suction alone holds the water up against gravity: ds/dz = 1, and suction equals height.
