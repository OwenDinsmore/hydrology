1 Model

Holes with radii drawn from an exponential of mean r̄ pass straight through a glass cylinder of radius R. Holes wider than 3r̄ are removed, not redrawn. A hole drains at the head where capillary rise fails, and only full holes conduct. With a unit gradient, each carries Poiseuille flow, so

r* = 2σ / (ρg|h|)(1)
θ(h) = Σr ≤ r* r² / R²,  ΘE = θ / φ(2)
K(h) = (ρg / 8μ) Σr ≤ r* r⁴ / R²(3)

Sample height cancels, and no tortuosity correction is applied, so b = 0.1

4 Discussion

A truly power-law pore distribution, ΘE ∝ rλ, gives K ∝ rλ+2 and so c = 1 + 2/λ exactly. This distribution is only a power law in its small pores, where λ → 3 and c → 5/3, which satisfies the relation (Figure 3, left). The large pores bend the curve, so one straight line cannot fit it, and both exponents depend on the fit range (Table 3).
